Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

PaisaFlow is a personal expense tracking app. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use the service, how we use it, where it is stored, and the choices you have. We keep things simple: the data you enter is yours, and we use it only to provide the service to you.

1. Data We Collect

To create an account and use PaisaFlow, we collect the following information:

  • Account information: the username and password you choose when registering, plus the email address associated with your account.
  • Expense data: the amount, category, date, and optional description you provide when logging an expense.
  • Session data: technical information such as your IP address, browser type, and session cookies required to keep you signed in.

We do not collect sensitive personal information such as government identification numbers, payment card details, or bank credentials. If you choose to include such information in free-text fields like an expense description, that is at your own discretion.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect solely to operate, maintain, and improve PaisaFlow. Specifically, we use your data to:

  • Authenticate you when you sign in and protect your account.
  • Store and display the expenses you log, including category breakdowns and date-range summaries.
  • Respond to support requests or account-related inquiries you send us.
  • Detect, prevent, and address security incidents or abuse of the service.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

3. Data Storage and Retention

Your account information and expense data are stored in a secure database that backs the PaisaFlow service. Passwords are stored in hashed form and are never stored or transmitted in plain text.

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may delete individual expenses at any time, and you may close your account to have your personal data removed from active storage. Backups may persist for a limited period as part of standard operational practices; they are deleted on a rolling basis.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. While we take reasonable steps to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Third Party Services

PaisaFlow relies on a small set of third-party services to operate. We share information with these providers only to the extent necessary to run the service, and only with vendors that respect the privacy of your data. The current categories are:

  • Hosting infrastructure: the provider that runs the servers and database that store your account and expense data.
  • Web fonts: Google Fonts is used to load the typography on the site; Google may receive your IP address and browser metadata when fonts are loaded.

We do not integrate advertising networks, analytics trackers, or social media pixels. If this changes, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent.

5. Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you. You can update most of your account information directly within the app, and you can delete expenses individually. If you would like to close your account or have any questions about your data, please contact us using the details below.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of PaisaFlow after a change has been posted indicates that you accept the updated policy.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out through the contact information available in the app. We will do our best to respond promptly.
